# Portfolio

Pramod Kadam's public professional page. Its readers are recruiters, who skim it, and data engineering hiring managers, who judge depth of work. He is not job-hunting, so the page states facts and makes no pitch.

## Language

**Intro strip**:
The full-screen illustrated scene at the top of the page: headline, and a summary with years of experience, employer and current work. A recruiter should be able to read it in seconds without scrolling.
_Avoid_: Hero, banner, hero panel

**Role**:
One period of employment at one employer: title, employer, level, dates. Roles are listed with the most recent first.
_Avoid_: Job, position, experience item

**Level**:
The employer's internal grade (e.g. Accenture "Senior Analyst"). It is shown under the title, never in place of it.
_Avoid_: Designation, band

**Case study**:
A specific piece of work done within a Role, shown as a card in Selected work (a one-liner and a confirmed figure) with the full case behind "Read the case". Every case study names its Role's employer and dates.
_Avoid_: Project, featured project

**Recognition**:
An award, shown as the closing card of Selected work and as a chip on its Role in the path. It is not tied to a specific piece of work unless Pramod confirms that is what it was for.
_Avoid_: Achievement, honour

**Personal project**:
Work done outside employment. None are shown at present; one will be added only if it is real data engineering work with public source code.
_Avoid_: Side project, hobby project
